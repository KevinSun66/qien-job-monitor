## Official JD summary

This is an entry-level data-engineering role supporting development, maintenance and optimization of data solutions on Databricks.

Core responsibilities:
- Build and maintain data pipelines and transformations using Databricks, SQL, Python and PySpark.
- Ingest and process structured and semi-structured data.
- Develop Databricks notebooks, workflows and jobs and build Delta Lake/lakehouse datasets.
- Support ETL/ELT across source systems, cloud storage and downstream applications.
- Validate data quality, monitor pipelines, troubleshoot failures and participate in root-cause analysis.
- Use Git and established CI/CD practices and document pipelines, models and transformation logic.
- Collaborate with teams supporting Finance, Supply Chain and Operations.

Required profile:
- Bachelor's degree in a related field, or equivalent education, technical training, certifications, internships or practical experience.
- 0–2 years in data engineering, software development, BI, analytics, data science or a related technical field.
- Foundational SQL, Python, Databricks or similar cloud data-platform knowledge; familiarity with Spark/PySpark, ETL/ELT, relational databases, data modeling, cloud concepts and Git.

Preferred exposure includes Delta Lake, Unity Catalog, AWS, Airflow, CI/CD, Power BI, Kafka, data warehouses and data lakes.
