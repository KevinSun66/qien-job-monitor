## AWS Emerging Talent Program - Solutions Architect

The Emerging Talent (ET) program within AWS Industries and Strategic Accounts develops the next generation of AWS Solutions Architects (SAs). The program prepares participants to work with AWS's most influential and strategic customers and help them transform their businesses on the AWS Cloud.

### Program Overview

The program offers a 6-month mentorship:
- Learn how to architect solutions on the AWS Cloud
- Develop skills to lead customer engagements as a Solutions Architect
- Work alongside experienced professionals who guide development

### Location Details

The program operates in Dallas, Texas or Seattle, WA for the first 6 months. After completing the program, participants are assigned to a customer account that may require relocation within the United States to a city with an Amazon office near the customer.

### Start Date Details

Cohort-based program. Tentative start dates: **January 4, 2027** and **July 6, 2027**.

### What You'll Do

- Engage directly with real customers on actual business challenges
- Participate in hands-on customer engagements from day one
- Apply AWS services and architectural best practices in real-world scenarios
- Develop technical and business acumen through direct customer interaction
- Work with mentors to build and implement solutions for actual business needs
- Prepare for a role advising AWS's most strategic and influential customers through practical experience

### Key Job Responsibilities

- Create solutions that address customer business problems and accelerate adoption of AWS services
- Interface with customer stakeholders, including developers and team leads
- Gather customer feedback and translate it into clear business and technical requirements for product and engineering teams

## About the Team

After completing the ET program, participants progress to field Solutions Architect roles, collaborating with sales account teams to drive AWS service adoption, communicate best practices, and relay customer feedback internally to help shape AWS Cloud direction.

## Basic Qualifications

- Experience with two or more of: networking fundamentals, security, storage or databases (relational or NoSQL), operating systems (Unix, Linux, and/or Windows)
- Experience communicating across technical and non-technical audiences, including executive-level stakeholders or clients
- Currently has, or is obtaining, a Bachelor's degree or above in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or related fields
- Experience communicating and delivering presentations to customers, stakeholders, and/or teammates
- Experience programming with at least one modern language such as Python, JavaScript, C#, Java, etc.
- Must be 18 or older

## Preferred Qualifications

- Experience implementing a cloud-based technology solution in a school project or while working for a company
- Experience in one or more of: analytics, security, storage, DevOps, application development, or machine learning
- AWS Associate-level certification(s) or above
