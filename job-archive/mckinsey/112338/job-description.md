## YOUR IMPACT (source PDF pp. 1–2)

Work as a core member of a multidisciplinary team to build and deliver advanced analytics and AI solutions to complex business challenges. Contribute to analytical workstreams, learn from experienced data scientists and engineers, and build expertise across industries and technologies.

Full-time opportunity for current students graduating between December 2026 and August 2027 with Bachelor's, Master's or PhD in computer science, machine learning, applied statistics, mathematics, engineering, artificial intelligence or related field.

Translate business challenges into analytical problems; develop and evaluate solutions in machine learning, optimization, GenAI and agentic AI. Select analytical approaches, apply data-quality and evaluation standards, and take increasing ownership of robust solutions.

Examples: agentic AI for insurance claims workflows; optimization of capital-project schedules and funding; predictive modeling and agentic AI for travel customer service; automotive supply-chain digital twin.

Collaborate with QuantumBlack and QuantumBlack Labs, data scientists, data engineers, software engineers and industry experts; engage client technical and business teams.

## YOUR GROWTH (source PDF pp. 2–3)

## YOUR QUALIFICATIONS AND SKILLS (source PDF pp. 3–4)

1. Upcoming graduation December 2026–August 2027 with Bachelor's/Master's/PhD in relevant technical or quantitative field.
2. Professional experience applying ML, statistical modeling, optimization or GenAI to business problems; **Python and SQL required**, including Python AI stack.
3. Translate business problems to analytical challenges; build/evaluate supervised, unsupervised, statistical or forecasting models with guidance.
4. Foundational optimization, simulation and decision logic.
5. Familiarity with GenAI tools and simple agentic AI frameworks; eagerness to apply LLMs to defined-task automation.
6. Validate work, document assumptions/limitations, evaluate prompts/RAG/agents; fairness, explainability and human oversight awareness.
7. Software engineering and MLOps/LLMOps best practices with guidance; introductory CI/CD and agentic engineering exposure.
8. Strong time management, willingness to travel, strong written and spoken English and local-office language communication.
