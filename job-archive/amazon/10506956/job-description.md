## Role and program

The AWS Industries and Strategic Accounts Emerging Talent program develops early-career Solutions Architects through six months of mentorship and direct customer work. Responsibilities include building AWS cloud architectures, implementing customer solutions, explaining technical designs, and advising strategic customers. Machine learning is one of the listed relevant domains, but assignment to a dedicated AI/ML account is not guaranteed.

## NG and start-date assessment
