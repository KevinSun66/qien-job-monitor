## Summary

The Applied Machine Learning team in Apple's AI and Data Platform organization builds and operates ML, GenAI, inference, and data platforms and services. The reliability engineer works on large-scale platform and distributed systems across hybrid-cloud environments, including GenAI, ML, inference, and big-data platforms.

## Minimum qualifications

- BS/MS in Computer Science or equivalent experience.
- 2+ years of programming experience in Python, Java, or Go.
- 2+ years of experience with Kubernetes, Docker, or another container-orchestration framework.

## Preferred qualifications

Open-source code reading, CI/CD, production troubleshooting, large-scale distributed-systems performance, uptime/availability, big-data technologies such as Spark/Flink/Iceberg, GenAI/ML technologies such as Ray/MLflow/model serving, and Linux/database/security concepts.
