## Official summary

Apple is seeking a motivated Software Engineer to build and operate reliable, secure and scalable cloud platforms and services. The role develops software and automation for production systems, Kubernetes, cloud infrastructure, observability, analytics and emerging AI/ML workloads. It works with experienced engineers, architects, SREs and AI/ML teams.

## Core responsibilities

- Build and maintain software, services, APIs, tools and automation in Python and Go.
- Develop cloud-native platform capabilities across production and non-production environments.
- Deploy, operate and troubleshoot Kubernetes services; automate deployment and platform operations.
- Improve availability, performance, scalability and operational efficiency.
- Use logs, metrics, traces, dashboards and alerts for troubleshooting and improvement.
- Analyze operational/application data for trends, anomalies and bottlenecks.
- Support AI/ML training infrastructure, LLM inference and GPU workloads.
- Apply LLMs and AI to software development, troubleshooting, analytics and operational workflows.
- Participate in capacity, scale, performance and disaster-recovery work.

## Minimum qualifications captured from the official JD

- Solid software-engineering fundamentals plus experience developing and maintaining production software, services, tools or automation.
- Python and/or Go proficiency.
- Hands-on Kubernetes and container experience, including application deployment and troubleshooting.
- Experience with AWS, Google Cloud or Azure.
- Familiarity with Terraform, Ansible or similar infrastructure automation.
- Understanding of monitoring, alerting, SLIs/SLOs, incident management, capacity planning and automation.
- Exposure to Prometheus, Grafana, Splunk, OpenTelemetry or similar tooling.
- Ability to analyze system/application data; SQL, Python analysis, dashboards or reporting are a plus.
- Working knowledge of distributed-systems concepts.

## Preferred qualifications

- Familiarity with AI/ML, LLMs, model inference or GPU workloads.
- Prior AI/ML infrastructure experience is beneficial but not required.
- Strong problem-solving, troubleshooting, communication and cross-functional collaboration.
