## Verified JD summary

Horizon3's Adversarial Intelligence Frontier Lab studies agentic systems and cybersecurity. The role conducts research and engineering on AI agents for security, autonomous penetration testing, attack-path reasoning, cyber deception, AI safety and security, and evaluation of AI systems.

Core responsibilities include:

- Formulate research questions from real security environments and customer problems.
- Design experiments and build research prototypes.
- Evaluate LLMs, agentic systems, reasoning methods and learning algorithms.
- Analyze security systems, attack paths, telemetry, graphs and configurations.
- Work with researchers, attack engineers, security engineers and product teams.
- Document and communicate methods, results and limitations.

Required profile:

- Strong computer-science foundations and programming ability.
- Technical depth in systems security, machine learning or a closely related area, plus interest in developing the complementary area.
- Ability to design experiments and analyze evidence.
- A degree in Computer Science or a related field, or equivalent technical experience.
- No minimum years of professional experience are stated.

Preferred evidence includes security or ML/LLM/agent experience, research through coursework, thesis, internship or comparable projects, experimental infrastructure, publications or open-source work. Publications are explicitly not required.

Travel may be up to 5%.
