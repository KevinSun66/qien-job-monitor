## Official JD summary

Abacus Insights describes itself as an AI and data platform for health plans. The Associate Data Engineer partners with clients, implementation teams and internal stakeholders to deliver data extracts and distribution solutions from its cloud data-management platform.

Core responsibilities:
- Analyze, profile and map data for client outputs.
- Build and maintain data-distribution extracts, SQL queries and scripts with senior guidance.
- Optimize extract scripts for performance, scalability and reliability.
- Validate outputs, investigate discrepancies and contribute to root-cause analysis.
- Document requirements, workflows, mappings and technical decisions.
- Participate in client meetings and suggest process improvements and automation.

Required profile:
- Recent graduate with a bachelor's or master's degree, or equivalent academic/project/internship/professional experience in data engineering, analytics, computer science, information systems or a related field.
- Strong SQL and exposure to data analysis, profiling, reporting, mapping or validation through coursework, internships, projects, research or work.
- Interest in healthcare data and ability to work with large/complex datasets.

Preferred, not required:
- AWS, Azure, Databricks, Spark or other cloud data platforms.
- Snowflake or similar cloud data warehouse.
- ETL, data pipelines, query optimization or performance tuning.

Benefits listed include unlimited PTO, work-from-anywhere flexibility, health coverage, equity, home-office allowance and monthly cell-phone allowance.
