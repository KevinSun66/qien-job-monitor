## Role summary

Entry-level Associate AI Engineer supporting design, development, deployment and optimization of AI, machine learning, analytics and data-driven solutions. The role works with AI Engineers, Data Engineers, Data Scientists, Solution Architects and business stakeholders and offers two specialization paths: AI Engineering and Data Engineering & Analytics.

## AI Engineering focus

- Develop AI, machine-learning and generative-AI applications.
- Build intelligent agents, conversational experiences, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) solutions and AI-assisted workflows.
- Develop application components, APIs, integrations and automation.
- Evaluate model performance, response quality, scalability and user experience.
- Test, monitor and optimize AI solutions.
- Learn modern AI frameworks, cloud AI services and software-engineering practices.

## Data Engineering & Analytics focus

- Prepare structured and unstructured data for AI and analytics.
- Use SQL, Python and platform tools for data querying and analysis.
- Perform exploratory analysis and data-quality work.
- Build data workflows, reusable scripts and pipelines.
- Prepare training data, features and evaluation datasets for ML/AI.
- Support data governance, quality and lineage.

## Required qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Information Systems, Engineering, Mathematics, Statistics or related discipline, or equivalent practical experience.
- 0–3 years in software engineering, AI, ML, data engineering, analytics or related technical fields.
- Working knowledge of Python or similar language.
- Basic software-development and problem-solving knowledge.
- Familiarity with databases, APIs, cloud technologies or modern development tools.
- Analytical, communication, collaboration and documentation skills.

## Preferred qualifications / technologies

Azure / Azure AI / Azure OpenAI / AWS / Google Cloud; AI/ML, GenAI, LLMs, retrieval systems and intelligent automation; SQL, ETL/ELT and data pipelines; Git, CI/CD, Docker, DevOps and MLOps; responsible AI, privacy, security and governance.
