## JD summary

CleanSlate Technology Group’s Data and AI Practice delivers production AI, machine-learning, data-platform, and cloud-modernization work for clients. The Associate AI/ML Engineer completes defined development, testing, evaluation, documentation, and production-delivery tasks with guidance from experienced engineers.

Responsibilities include:

- build and test components of AI workflows, services, and integrations;
- contribute to generative-AI and agent-based capabilities;
- run established tests and evaluations and document model limitations;
- support production delivery alongside data and application engineers;
- follow responsible-AI guidance for data use, bias, transparency, and privacy;
- write maintainable code, participate in reviews, and document results.
