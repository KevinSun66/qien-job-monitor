## Official JD summary

CleanSlate Technology Group’s Data and AI Practice delivers production AI, machine-learning, data-platform, and cloud-modernization work for clients. The Associate AI/ML Engineer completes defined development, testing, evaluation, documentation, and production-delivery tasks with guidance from experienced engineers.

Responsibilities include:

- complete development tasks for client AI/ML solutions, including generative-AI and agent-based capabilities;
- build and test components of AI workflows, services, and integrations;
- run established tests and evaluations and document model limitations or data-quality concerns;
- support production delivery alongside data and application engineers;
- follow responsible-AI guidance for data use, bias, transparency, and privacy;
- write maintainable code, participate in code reviews, and document implementation and test results.

## Entry requirements

The official posting asks for foundational hands-on AI/ML experience and explicitly allows professional work, internships, coursework, research, or personal projects. It also asks for foundational Python, structured or unstructured data preparation, Git, exposure to multistage deployment, familiarity with AWS/Databricks/Azure, data-pipeline and data-quality concepts, and clear technical communication. It does not set a one-year or higher minimum.

Preferred qualifications include exposure to LLM platforms such as Amazon Bedrock or Azure AI Foundry, agentic tooling and development assistants, MLOps, data-engineering collaboration, and relevant cloud or data-platform certifications.
