## Role summary

Caterpillar is hiring an AI Automation Engineer to build annotation automation for robotics, autonomy, computer vision and Vision-Language-Action (VLA) systems. The role combines software engineering, machine learning and data engineering to create production auto-labeling, auto-captioning, human-review and AI-driven quality-assurance workflows.

## Responsibilities

- Develop and deploy automated labeling and captioning for perception and VLA datasets.
- Train, fine-tune and evaluate models for auto-labeling, auto-captioning and annotation quality automation.
- Build pipelines spanning automated labeling, human review, quality validation and dataset publication.
- Extend NVIDIA Cosmos Curator, Cosmos Reason and related platforms.
- Integrate Caterpillar data systems with external annotation providers and human-in-the-loop workflows.
- Maintain reliability, scalability, observability and operational excellence.

## Basic qualifications

- Bachelor's in Computer Science, Software Engineering, AI, ML, Robotics or a related field.
- Production-grade Python software experience, modern engineering practices and Docker/Kubernetes.
- Distributed systems, APIs and data-processing-pipeline experience.
- Experience training, fine-tuning or deploying ML models.
- Camera/LiDAR dataset creation and ground-truth experience.
- Annotation QA and dataset-management tools such as Labelbox, Supervisely or Roboflow.
- ML tooling such as PyTorch, TensorFlow, ONNX, Weights & Biases or MLflow.

## Preferred qualifications

Computer vision, robotics, autonomy, Physical AI, multimodal AI, AI-assisted annotation, foundation/vision/multimodal models, NVIDIA Cosmos, VLA workflows, sensor data, cloud/MLOps, ML deployment and human-in-the-loop systems.
